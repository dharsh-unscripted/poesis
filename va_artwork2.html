Still Life Artwork

  • By Hannah Yoon
  • Visual Arts

Hannah Yoon is a burgeoning artist. She is interested in experimenting with many different art styles. One of her preferred styles is Still Life. To her, still life represents an opportunity to slow down and see the intricate details in seemingly mundane items. Her work is a culmination of common items executed with great attention to detail.
