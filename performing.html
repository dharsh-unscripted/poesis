Performing Arts

Performing arts are not simply about entertainment[cite: 1]. It's an embodied form of learning, where students discover that knowledge lives in gesture, movement, and voice[cite: 1]. Through theater, dance, and music performance, students learn to communicate beyond words and to collaborate in real time, attuning themselves to others in ways that require trust and vulnerability[cite: 1]. We live in a world increasingly mediated by screens and digital interaction[cite: 1]. Thus, there is inherent value in learning to be fully present in physical space, to take creative risks in front of others, and to understand that our bodies carry meaning[cite: 1].
