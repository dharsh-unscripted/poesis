Selected Poems by Maya Salles

  • By Maya Salles

Maya Salles is a rising senior at Queens Collegiate: A College Board School, located in Jamaica, Queens, NYC. She is a native New Yorker who began writing in 2023 at a bonfire when inspiration struck. She is a poet, theater lover, and woman who isn’t afraid to speak up even if no one else is.

Woman

Inspired by “The Dinner Party” at the Brooklyn Museum.

Wing One
The beginning of woman
the start of recognition not recognized until recent
With every passing breath,
With every forward glide,
the story of ancestry leads
i am woman
forward facing
womb making
strong souled woman
an intellect of emotional direction
a leader with a love to be led
i am woman
My palms ache with the life’s before
my legs weary of the path ahead
my body still strong
still holding in nurture
still trekking through forests of ancestral power
i am woman
i am curated and complexly illustrated through the hands of every woman who stood grounded
in yesterday
today
tomorrow
and forever.
I am woman as woman is me
i am her as she is me
we are one and one are we.

Mother

This poem is inspired by the life of a shadow who attempts to run from the dark. The story of “the apple does not fall far from the tree”—a saying I grew up hearing.

Ma holds grudges for decades on end
ma dances in love and leaves when she wins
ma has a forced field layered with skin
ma is scary
ma is beautiful and intuitive
ma believes in spirits but not in her own kid
ma holds things but not hands only fists
ma doesnt hug only smiles or smirks
ma has a force field
A life of much control
an existence undeniable for every message is easily disproved
ma is natural superstitious mother
one of much verbal knowledge
one of much voice but no thought
ma is rude
she speaks and the room goes silent
ma is direct
she speaks and all else just follow
everyday i find myself chasing tomorrow with today
losing the reality that while running… mother remains
my eyes hold grudges
this heart loves to love
this voice some find scary and intimidating
my hands are always too cold
some find i am untouchable although i seek only to touched by love
some find this voice rude
some go quiet when i speak
some are anxious with me around
others are trying to figure me out
maybe everyday mother gets closer to me
maybe i am mother as she swears i am her and she is me.

Random Words of Intent

This piece labeled “random words of intent” developed from a writing exercise of taking 30 words and creative 1 piece with it. The words are in bold and show how when words mesh, beauty arises with the power to call action on the corrupt and the beautiful.

Take my breathe like a Storm shooting bullets of
Exhausted Lightning in a land full of supposed freedom
This nation is Gloomy
the world remains lacking Cozy
Even the pathways of school hallways are Wet with the
BOOM of anger
anger layed out creating a
“beautiful painting”
Take my breath away like we the Tea we stole and flushed in effort to be different
Take it like how being Free was taken from the ancestors of the Wind
take my breath like out George Floyd, the marches after, the march on Washington, the
individual Loud deaths took the breaths out all those who lie dead in the graveyard of political
"rebellion"
take my breath and take my light
Illumination maybe flicker but wont ever dissipate
Take my heart and silence my voice
take my breath and all of my land
just remember the Rain and the Thunder is no mere whisper
rather a Cloud of intentional ancestral power
Remember my breath when you step in a Puddle
See the lives when the weather gets Foggy
Try to hide under a man made Umbrella
Hide from the Cold
Tell tales using Peppa (pig) to your children
tell them the Oak covered individuals are Dark spirit
take my breath replace it with Judgement
Take my Boot(s)
and pretend your in power
dance in the rain and tell your kids to make a Splash
Just remember when Overcast comes
And the Frogs stop jumping
When the sky ages Grey
The Poncho no longer protects
When the Jacket is a mere style of insecurity
Remember to look up
my breath
the breath of the past
the ancestors before will line the sky with Rainbow
reminding you of all the lost before.

Time Tells Tales

Poem labeled “time tells tales” written in a moment of “self love” and transitioned in self awareness. Writing has no limit except for the one you place on yourself. This piece challenges time with the concept of social interaction.
Photo taken July 2025

I think time tells tales
Like lying about my life when your life is nothing like mine
I stand here
The patter of water on my back
Eyes open to the shades of gray
The hues of gray that tell a story I can’t explain because yet again as stare the narrative
changes
And as I exist the store becomes frameless
Time tells tales of
Who I am to who I was
Like standing there 20 minutes ago with hair dripping attempting to tempt my mental into active
sustain
I did this last year too
I stood in this shower last year just as I do this year but yet the year isn’t what changed but
rather the psychic analytical ignorance that altered
Time tells tales
The tales about my spirit of independence
Looking at me like I’m special yet the only special things is the fact of trauma layered creating
every cell of skin on my lying existence
Tales about what I must’ve done
Tales on who I should’ve become
Lies that lie my body into the grave the 13 year old me dug out for when this day would
eventually arrive
Don’t get me wrong
I don’t seek to die
Nor seek to run
I’ve been running all my life and that’s why I shall yell above the clouds, beneath the soil, and
inside the hearts of every soul the reality that
Time tells lies…
Tales
Untrue
Fact twisted story’s about the change that when u stand in the doorway of any shower shed
from the surface to reveal the reality
(Clap)
The layer is loose
My skin itching as a demon seeks to reveal some story that i inhaled with every breath
My heart beating as though father were here
Mind running but I said I don don’t seek to run
I’ve been running all my life
The sponge not strong enough
The soap not strong enough
The water not strong enough
The brush
Heat
Scrub not strong enough
My guard just strong enough
(Clap)
I tell you
Time tells tales like I lie to myself
Tales of lies I’ve never been and wish to never be
Lies I’ve told myself put into extreme perspective and told to you
Time tells tales about life.
