Language Arts

Multilingual learning is crucial as it is a pathway to deeper thinking and more pronounced cultural understanding. Every language has its own means of expression that are deeply rooted in the nation’s culture and history. Multilingual education allows individuals to become more aware of their surroundings and allows them to navigate differences with patience and respect. Moreover, placing language arts as a core part of education positions language as a bridge rather than a barrier, as it is often perceived to be, motivating students to travel across cultures with ingrained curiosity and responsibility.
