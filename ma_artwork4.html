THE OCEAN’S DAUGHTER

  • By Yunnie
  • Media Arts

Yunnie Ha has helped illustrate a children’s book titled The Ocean’s Daughter. The book follows the Haenyeo protest in Jeju-do during the Japanese occupation in Korea. The beautiful and captivating illustrations allow younger audiences to learn about Korea’s history in a visual manner. Here are a few snapshots of the book.
