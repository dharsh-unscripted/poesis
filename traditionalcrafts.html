Traditional Crafts

  1. Home
  2. Traditional Crafts

Traditional cultural arts are not relics of the past but living practices that carry forward the wisdom and aesthetic traditions of communities across generations. Through weaving, pottery, indigenous storytelling, folk music, and ceremonial dance, students discover that creation is inseparable from cultural identity and community. We live in a world of rapid consumption and disposable culture. There's inherent value in learning to work with our hands and recognizing that every object and practice carries the memory of those who came before us. Traditional arts position students as both inheritors and innovators, carrying forward cultural legacies while adapting them to the present with integrity and reverence.
