Small Choice. Big Outcome.

By Hannah Yoon

Hannah Yoon is a fledgling artist. The following art pieces are a representation of an important life lesson. One small choice can make a big difference. For instance, within chess one move can change the outcome of the entire game, similarly with billiards. The same goes for cards and gambling, a moment’s choice or a simple hesitation can cause one to become a victor basking in glory or a dejected soul leaving the game. These pieces ultimately explicate how small actions create a big effect on the course of one’s life.
